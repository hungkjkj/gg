TOOL TRADE MT5 - Cai dat tren Windows

1. Giai nen toan bo file TOOL-TRADE-Installer.zip.
2. Chay Install.bat va cho bo cai dat hoan tat.
3. Ung dung se mo sau khi cai dat; co the mo lai bang shortcut TOOL TRADE tren Desktop.

May cai dat khong can Python hoac Node.js. Bo cai dat se tu cai WebView2 Runtime neu may chua co va co ket noi Internet.

Can co MetaTrader 5 cua broker, dang mo va dang nhap tai khoan de lay du lieu thi truong. TOOL TRADE khong cai MT5 vi moi broker co bo cai dat va may chu dang nhap rieng.

DataServer.mq5 trong thu muc EA la thanh phan tuy chon, chi can cai vao MT5 neu ban dung co che du phong.

Cau hinh, canh bao, chien luoc va nhat ky duoc luu trong:
%LOCALAPPDATA%\TOOL TRADE

Neu can cai WebView2 Runtime thu cong, dung trang tai chinh thuc cua Microsoft:
https://developer.microsoft.com/microsoft-edge/webview2/
